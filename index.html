Why teams use it

Everything runs in your browser.

No account, no install, no server. Your projects, your library, and your API key all stay on your device. The only thing that ever leaves it is the render request you trigger yourself.

40+ item library

Displays, kiosks, staging, truss, lighting, and furniture across 13 categories. Every item resizes freely, so one icon covers every real-world size instead of a fixed SKU.

One-click AI render

Send the layout to Gemini and get back a photorealistic booth photo with correct shadows, materials, and lighting, while every item stays exactly where you put it.

Auto bill of materials

Every item on the canvas is tallied by name and category automatically. No manual counting and no separate spreadsheet to keep in sync.

Drag, tap, pinch

Drag items on desktop, tap to add on a phone or tablet, pinch to zoom. Rotate, flip, resize and undo. It behaves like a real design tool on any screen.

Autosaves as you work

Every change is saved to your browser automatically. Close the tab, come back later, and your project is exactly where you left it.

Installable, works offline

Add it to your home screen or desktop. Once loaded, the layout tool keeps working with no connection. Only AI renders need the internet.
